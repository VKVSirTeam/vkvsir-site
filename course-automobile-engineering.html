Automobile Engineering

A complete 36-hour course (about 6 weeks) covering the modern vehicle from chassis to air-conditioning, in easy Hindi & English with animations.

Why this subject matters

  • ✔ The core subject for Mechanical and Automobile students in Diploma, B.Tech and AMIE programmes.
  • ✔ Directly useful for RTO / MVI Inspector, GATE, PSU and automotive industry interviews.
  • ✔ Builds the foundation for electric and hybrid vehicle technology.

What you will learn

  • Chassis, frames and automotive materials
  • Clutches, gearboxes, automatic transmission and differentials
  • Brakes, steering geometry and suspension systems
  • Wheels, tyres, axles and drive layouts
  • Battery, starting, charging, ignition and lighting systems
  • Automotive air-conditioning and refrigerants
