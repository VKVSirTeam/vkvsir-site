Casting Process

Metal casting from pattern to finished part: moulding, melting, pouring and defect control.

Why this subject matters

  • ✔ A core manufacturing process asked in university, GATE and PSU exams.
  • ✔ Widely used in automotive and heavy industry.
  • ✔ Links materials science with production.

What you will learn

  • Patterns and allowances
  • Moulding sands and methods
  • Gating and risering
  • Special casting processes and defects
