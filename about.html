About VKVSir

Dr. Vinod Kumar Verma (Ph.D., M.Tech., MBA): professor, innovator, consultant and mentor, with international industry experience in Dubai and Afghanistan.

Profile

Dr. Vinod Kumar Verma has served as a faculty member in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Engineering College Ajmer since 2008. He is recognised for his expertise in Automobile Engineering, Renewable Energy, Marketing, and New Product Design & Launching.

Education

Diploma topper (Govt. Polytechnic, Kota); B.E. (MBM Engineering College, Jodhpur); M.Tech. (IIT Jodhpur); Ph.D.; and MBA in Marketing (Symbiosis, Pune).

Global industry experience

More than five years in industry: as a Project Manager on international projects in Dubai and Afghanistan, and as a General Manager in Indian industry. Over seventeen years in teaching, with a mission to empower the young generation through quality education and meaningful opportunities.

Awards & recognition

Honoured and appreciated by the Former President of India, Late Shri Pranab Mukherjee; the Union MHRD Minister, Dr. Satyapal Singh; the Education Minister of Rajasthan; the District Collector of Ajmer; and many other dignitaries.

Consultancy & expert roles

  • Consultancy work delivered for various industries and government projects.
  • Expert in examination and evaluation for statutory bodies including RPSC, Rajasthan Board, MPUAT, RTU Kota, the University of Rajasthan, etc.

Milestones

  • Granted patent on an automatic fire-extinguishing and window-breaking system for cars.
  • Publications in reputed national and international journals.
  • Founder of SAEINDIA at ECA; team reached the BAJA main round.
  • Organised 60+ seminars, industrial tours and workshops; invited expert and keynote speaker.

More about VKVSir

Where education, innovation and mentorship meet

Welcome to the official website of Dr. Vinod Kumar Verma, affectionately known as VKVSir, a respected name in automobile engineering, manufacturing processes and innovation.

With more than two decades of combined corporate and academic experience, Dr. Verma brings practical insight to every classroom. His five years in industry, leading international projects as a Project Manager in Dubai and Afghanistan and later serving as a General Manager in Indian industry, gave him a first-hand understanding of real-world engineering, people and business challenges.

What truly sets him apart is his passion for teaching and innovation. Over more than seventeen years in academia, he has guided countless students towards their academic and professional goals. His commitment to innovation is reflected in his research publications, a granted patent, and consultancy work delivered for various industries and government projects.

Beyond the classroom, Dr. Verma is valued as a mentor and motivator. Students regularly seek his advice on careers and personal growth, drawn by his genuine wish to see them succeed. As a coordinator of the Training and Placement Cell, he bridges academia and industry by building partnerships with leading companies and HR professionals. He also serves statutory bodies such as RPSC, RTU Kota, MPUAT, the Rajasthan Board and the University of Rajasthan as an expert in examination and evaluation.

His academic journey spans a B.E. from MBM Engineering College, Jodhpur, an M.Tech. from the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, a Ph.D., and an MBA from Symbiosis, Pune.

An active voice on LinkedIn, Dr. Verma shares his knowledge and mentorship with learners and professionals worldwide.

We invite you to explore the world of Dr. Vinod Kumar Verma, where education, innovation and mentorship come together to shape the leaders of tomorrow.
