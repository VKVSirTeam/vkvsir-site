Manufacturing Processes

Foundry, casting, welding, forming and powder metallurgy: how engineering parts are actually made, from fundamentals to advanced processes.

Why this subject matters

  • ✔ A core subject for Mechanical, Production and Industrial engineering in Diploma, B.Tech and AMIE.
  • ✔ Heavily asked in GATE, ESE, PSU and state engineering service exams.
  • ✔ Directly applicable on the shop floor, in quality and in process engineering roles.

What you will learn

  • Patterns, moulding sand, cores, gating and risering
  • Sand, die, centrifugal, investment and other casting processes
  • Gas, arc, TIG, MIG, resistance and special welding processes
  • Forging, rolling, extrusion, drawing and press work
  • Powder metallurgy, defects and their remedies, and much more
